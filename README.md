# Cruisin' the Heimy

An arcade checkpoint racer for the browser. Ten cars on the grid, a clock that only checkpoints can top up, and two
point-to-point tracks: a dirt road through prehistory where the dinosaurs have right of way, and a holiday coast
highway at sunset with traffic coming the other way. Pick a car, pick automatic or a four-speed manual, and fight up
from the back of the pack.
**Play it at https://demidummy.github.io/cruisin-the-heimy/**

Keyboard, gamepad, or touch controls on a phone held sideways. The controls are listed under Options.

This repo only holds the built game. It's published by `tools/release-pages.sh`.
